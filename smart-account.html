What Is JosJis Smart Account?

JosJis Smart Account is a planned blockchain account based on smart contract technology. Unlike a regular wallet, its permissions can be managed through programmable rules.

The goal is to allow users to manage assets through an authorized Controller while keeping the Smart Account address and its assets unchanged when the Controller is replaced.

How It Works

01. Connect Wallet

Connect a compatible wallet such as MetaMask. The wallet signs requests without sharing its seed phrase with the website.

02. Verify Permissions

The Smart Account checks whether the connected wallet is authorized as its active Controller.

03. Manage Account

An authorized Controller can perform permitted operations according to the Smart Account rules.

04. Replace Controller

The current Controller can authorize a new wallet to take over account management, subject to contract rules.

Future Recovery System

Recovery Is Not Available Yet

Recovery is intentionally postponed for the initial version. Its permissions and security rules will be designed separately before implementation.

Security Considerations

Important Security Notice

The Smart Account will depend on its Controller permissions. If the Controller loses access to its private key and no recovery mechanism exists, the account may become permanently inaccessible.

Seed Phrase Protection

JosJis will never ask users to enter their wallet seed phrase into the website.

Wallet Authorization

Account operations will require valid authorization from the connected wallet.

Permission Verification

Controller permissions must be verified before any protected operation is executed.
