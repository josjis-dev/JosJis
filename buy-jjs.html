OFFICIAL JOSJIS GUIDE

HOW TO BUY JOSJIS ($JJS)

Welcome to JosJis! Follow this simple guide to learn how to buy JosJis tokens using MetaMask on the Polygon network.

Before You Start

Before purchasing JosJis, make sure you have:

  • A MetaMask mobile wallet.
  • Access to the Polygon network.
  • POL tokens to swap and cover network gas fees.
  • A working internet connection.

Step-by-Step Buying Guide

1

Install MetaMask

Download the official MetaMask mobile application from Google Play Store or Apple App Store.

Create a new wallet or import an existing wallet if you already have one.

Keep your Secret Recovery Phrase private. Never share it with anyone.

2

Select Polygon Network

Open MetaMask and select the Polygon network.

Make sure your wallet has enough POL for the swap and network transaction fees.

3

Open MetaMask Swap

Open the Swap feature in your MetaMask mobile wallet.

Select POL as the token you want to swap from, or another supported token available in your wallet.

Select JJS as the token you want to receive.

4

Find the Official JJS Token

In the token selection field, search for JosJis or paste the official JJS contract address below.

0x52C8Ff13ada1431376b3E77E8219b644805122dF

Verify that the token contract matches the official address before continuing.

You do not need to import the token into your wallet just to select it for a swap.

5

Enter the Amount

Enter the amount of POL or another supported token you want to swap for JJS.

Review the estimated amount of JJS you will receive.

Check the exchange rate, price impact, network fee, and any displayed swap fees.

6

Review and Confirm Your Swap

Carefully review all transaction details displayed by MetaMask.

  • Confirm the correct JJS token.
  • Check the amount you will receive.
  • Review the estimated transaction fees.
  • Check the minimum received amount if displayed.

If everything is correct, confirm the transaction in MetaMask.

7

Check Your JJS Balance

Wait until the transaction is confirmed on the Polygon network.

Your JJS tokens should appear in your wallet after the successful swap.

If the balance is not displayed, you may manually add the official JJS token to MetaMask using its contract address.

Important Safety Information

  • Always verify the official JJS contract address before making a transaction.
  • Never share your Secret Recovery Phrase or private keys.
  • Only use the official JosJis website and trusted wallet applications.
  • Cryptocurrency transactions are generally irreversible once confirmed.
  • Swap availability, exchange rates, liquidity, price impact, and fees may change.
  • Never spend more than you can afford to lose.
