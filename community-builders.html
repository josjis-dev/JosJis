INTRODUCTION

A Community That Builds

A blockchain ecosystem becomes more meaningful when people create useful things with it. JosJis welcomes independent builders who want to experiment, develop, and introduce practical ways to use JJS.

You do not need to be an experienced blockchain developer. Start with a simple idea, a useful service, or a product that can accept JJS.
