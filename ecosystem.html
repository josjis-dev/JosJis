INTRODUCTION

Building Beyond a Token

JosJis is designed to grow beyond a single digital token. Its long-term vision is to develop an interconnected blockchain ecosystem where technology, utilities, and community participation can work together.

The ecosystem will be developed gradually, beginning with the JJS token foundation and expanding into additional blockchain services and infrastructure.
