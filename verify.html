Waiting for Safe owner approvals.

This feature must be opened inside Safe Wallet as a Safe App. Safe owners must approve the message according to the wallet's signing requirements. A message is not fully signed until the required approvals are collected.

Verify a signed message

Verification checks whether the Safe contract accepts the submitted signature. The exact message, signature format, Safe address and network must match. A valid signature proves authorization by the Safe, not the truth of the message's contents.
