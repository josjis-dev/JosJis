STAKING REWARDS

JJS Reward System

Rewards are calculated from the duration configuration stored in the staking smart contract.

Reward rates are fixed amounts for the selected staking period, not annual percentage yields. Rewards depend on available funded reward reserves.

About JosJis Staking

Staking uses a separate smart contract from the official JosJis token contract.

Never send JJS directly to a contract address. Always use the official staking interface.

Blockchain transactions require POL for network gas fees. Staked tokens are subject to the selected lock period.
