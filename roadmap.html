THE JOSJIS DEVELOPMENT JOURNEY

JosJis Roadmap

A gradual development journey focused on building a sustainable blockchain ecosystem powered by JosJis ($JJS).

Our Development Direction

Building step by step, with a long-term vision.

The JosJis roadmap outlines the project's development direction, beginning with the JJS token foundation and progressing toward digital utilities, cross-chain expansion, market infrastructure, and future blockchain development.

● Deployed ● Planned ● Research ● Long-Term Vision

Development Phases

The planned evolution of the JosJis ecosystem.

FOUNDATION DEPLOYED

Phase 1 — Token Foundation

Establishing the core JosJis token and its initial blockchain presence.

  • Deploy JosJis ($JJS) on Polygon.
  • Establish fixed token supply.
  • Verify the smart contract on PolygonScan.
  • Establish official project website.
  • Establish official community channels.
PLANNED

Phase 2 — Utility Development

Developing practical utility features for JJS holders and community members.

  • Develop flexible JJS staking.
  • Introduce utility-based staking benefits.
  • Develop seven membership NFT levels.
  • Build wallet connection and balance display.
  • Develop a staking dashboard.
PLANNED

Phase 3 — Multi-Chain Expansion

Exploring the expansion of JJS to additional EVM-compatible networks.

  • Research cross-chain architecture.
  • Develop JosJis Multi-Chain Bridge.
  • Explore Base integration.
  • Explore BNB Chain integration.
  • Explore Arbitrum integration.
  • Implement cross-chain security mechanisms.
RESEARCH

Phase 4 — JGPS Development

Researching a global reference system for JJS market information.

  • Design JJS Global Price System (JGPS).
  • Explore market data aggregation.
  • Research price reference methodology.
  • Explore multi-market data monitoring.
  • Develop safeguards for outdated market data.
LONG-TERM DEVELOPMENT

Phase 5 — DeFi Infrastructure

Exploring additional decentralized applications and blockchain services within the JosJis ecosystem.

  • Research decentralized applications.
  • Explore additional JJS utility services.
  • Study blockchain infrastructure.
  • Evaluate security and scalability requirements.
LONG-TERM VISION

Phase 6 — JosJis Network

Exploring the possibility of building an independent JosJis blockchain network.

  • Research EVM-compatible network architecture.
  • Study validator and consensus mechanisms.
  • Explore network economics and infrastructure.
  • Evaluate the feasibility of an independent network.

Our Development Principles

How we approach the growth of JosJis.

Transparency

Communicating project progress and development status clearly.

Security First

Prioritizing testing, smart contract security, and responsible implementation.

Gradual Development

Building and testing each component before expanding into more complex systems.

Community Participation

Encouraging community feedback and participation throughout development.

Roadmap Disclaimer

This roadmap represents the development direction and long-term vision of JosJis. Planned features and future infrastructure are not necessarily available or operational.

Development priorities, technical designs, and timelines may change based on testing, resources, security considerations, and project progress.

No specific launch dates or financial returns are guaranteed.

Building the Future of JosJis

From a single Polygon token to a connected blockchain ecosystem, JosJis aims to grow through learning, innovation, transparency, and community participation.
