Overview

JosJis Liquidity Center is a community-focused information page designed to encourage and educate users about providing liquidity to the JosJis trading pool.

The page provides pool information, liquidity statistics, and guidance for users who want to participate in the JosJis ecosystem through decentralized liquidity provision.

Important Risk Information

Providing liquidity involves risks, including:

- Impermanent loss.
- Price volatility.
- Smart contract risks.
- Liquidity range risks.
- Trading fees are variable and not guaranteed.

Users should understand these risks before providing liquidity.

JosJis does not guarantee profits or returns from liquidity provision.
