IMPORTANT INFORMATION

Understand the Risks

Providing liquidity involves financial risks. These may include impermanent loss, price volatility, smart contract risks, and concentrated liquidity range risks.

Trading fees are variable and not guaranteed. A position outside its selected price range may stop earning trading fees.

Always do your own research and only provide assets you can afford to risk.
